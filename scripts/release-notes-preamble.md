**Coming from Loudini 0.5.1 or older?** Update by hand once:

1. Choose **Quit Loudini** from its menu.
2. Download `Loudini.dmg` below, open it and drag Loudini into Applications, replacing the old copy.
3. Open Loudini from Applications.

From then on Loudini updates itself with one click in its menu. Since 0.4.1 you also get:

- **No more silence after opening the lid.** The overnight audio restarts are gone.
- **Your interface keeps its own volume.** Switching back to a DAC or audio interface brings back the level you left it at.
- **One-click updates** from the Loudini menu.
